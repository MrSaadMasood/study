8-Week Interview Preparation — Revised Master Schedule

Target: Senior Full Stack (MERN + Go) | 4–5 YOE | Pakistan + International Remote

Weekdays: 1 hr/day Saturday: 2–3 hrs Sunday: REST — no study Total: ~56–64 hrs over 8 weeks

Why this version is more followable: Single focused topic per weekday session. No parallel "daily habits" stacked on top of each other. Saturdays are the only heavy day. Sundays are protected rest. Every week has a clear theme so if work gets bad one week, you know exactly what to defer.
Document Set: The other 7 files contain the deep content for each section. This schedule tells you when to study what. Open the relevant file when a topic appears in your daily plan.

Weekly Structure (Fixed Pattern)

DayWhat HappensDuration
MondayMain subject study — Concept reading + notes1 hr
TuesdayMain subject study — Deeper topic OR related concept1 hr
WednesdayMain subject study — Final topic of the week's theme1 hr
ThursdayDSA — 1 pattern study + 1 problem solved completely1 hr
FridayDSA — 1 problem solved completely (harder one)1 hr
SaturdaySystem Design deep session OR Mock Interview2–3 hrs
Sunday — FULL REST. No studying. Recovery is part of the plan.
What "1 hr" means: Sit down, eliminate distractions, study the topic from the relevant document, and write 3–5 bullet points in your own words at the end. If you don't write it in your own words, you haven't internalized it. 1 focused hour beats 2 distracted hours every time.

What "2–3 hrs Saturday" means: If you have energy: 3 hrs. If life is busy: 2 hrs is the minimum to still complete a meaningful system design session. Below 2 hrs: reschedule to the following week's Saturday (push next week's topic to the weekend after).

After Week 8 — Continuation Plan (While Actively Interviewing)

Once Week 8 is done, you shift from structured prep to ongoing prep while interviewing. The goal changes: instead of covering new ground, you're (1) patching gaps exposed by real interviews and (2) deepening topics that keep coming up.

Continue Immediately (Week 9+)

  • 1 LeetCode Medium per weekday — 20–25 min warmup before work. This replaces the structured DSA sessions. Keep the habit alive throughout the job search.
  • Real interview debrief — After every real interview, write 3 questions you couldn't answer well. Study those topics the next day. Real interviews are the best prep data.
  • 1 new system design per Saturday — Until you've covered all 5 problems in doc 01 (real-time chat, file upload, payment system are still outstanding). Then invent your own: design Instagram, design Google Docs, design Uber's surge pricing.

If a Week Goes Badly — Minimum Viable Week

If work is brutal and you can only do the absolute minimum:
  1. Do Thursday's DSA problem (1 hr). This keeps the habit alive.
  2. Read the "Quick Reference" table from the doc relevant to that week's theme (20 min).
  3. Do Saturday's system design session if possible, even if only 1.5 hrs instead of 2–3.
That's 2.5 hrs for the whole week. Not ideal, but it keeps the momentum alive and doesn't break the schedule. The following week, continue from where you left off — don't try to "catch up" by doubling sessions, which leads to burnout.
