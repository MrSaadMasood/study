10-Week Interview Preparation

Target: Senior full stack, MERN, 4–5 years. Pakistan and international remote. Not a FAANG Hard-problem track.

Weekdays: 1 hr/day Saturday: 2–3 hrs Sunday: REST — no study Weeks 3–10 still to do: ~56–64 hrs

Start at Week 3. Weeks 1 and 2 are unchanged because they are already done. One topic per weekday. Saturday is the long day. Sunday is off. If a week breaks, do Thursday and Saturday, and do not catch up on Sunday.
Document Set: The other 7 files contain the deep content for each section. This schedule tells you when to study what. Open the relevant file when a topic appears in your daily plan.

Weekly Structure (Fixed Pattern)

DayWhat HappensDuration
MondayMain subject study — Concept reading + notes1 hr
TuesdayMain subject study — Deeper topic OR related concept1 hr
WednesdayMain subject study — Final topic of the week's theme1 hr
ThursdayDSA — 1 pattern study + 1 problem solved completely1 hr
FridayDSA — 1 problem solved completely (harder one)1 hr
SaturdaySystem Design deep session OR Mock Interview2–3 hrs
Sunday — FULL REST. No studying. Recovery is part of the plan.
What "1 hr" means: Sit down, eliminate distractions, study the topic from the relevant document, and write 3–5 bullet points in your own words at the end. If you don't write it in your own words, you haven't internalized it. 1 focused hour beats 2 distracted hours every time.

What "2–3 hrs Saturday" means: If you have energy: 3 hrs. If life is busy: 2 hrs is the minimum to still complete a meaningful system design session. Below 2 hrs: reschedule to the following week's Saturday (push next week's topic to the weekend after).

After Week 10

The plan is finished. Do not start Kubernetes, a Go course, GraphQL, or LeetCode Hard because the calendar ended. Use interviews as the syllabus.
  • After each real interview, write the questions you could not answer. Study the worst one the next day, for one hour.
  • If you want a coding warm-up, re-solve a problem from this list. Do not open the doc 04 bank unless an interview asked for that pattern.
  • A new design (file upload, payments, or anything else) only after a company has asked for that shape of system.

If a Week Goes Badly

  1. Do Thursday's problem (1 hr).
  2. Do Saturday at 2 hours if you can. Keep the closed-book part.
  3. Do not add Sunday, and do not double the next week.
